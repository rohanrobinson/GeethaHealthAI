1. Records tab. The user starts the import by tapping "Import from Apple Health". Nothing is read from HealthKit before this.
2. iOS presents Apple's Health Records sharing sheet.
3. Step 1 of 4: the user chooses which provider accounts to share from.
4. Step 2 of 4: Apple's "You Should Know" notice.
5. Step 3 of 4: our usage description, privacy policy link, and read-only access per category. The user turns on only the categories they want.
6. Step 4 of 4: how new records are shared. The default is "Ask Before Sharing".
7. Back in Geetha Health: a preview of the records Health shared. The user picks which ones to import. "Records stay on this device."
8. After import, the records appear on the Records tab alongside manually entered records.
9. Imported conditions.
10. Tapping a record opens it for viewing and editing. Records can also be deleted by swiping.
11. Imported medications.
12. Imported allergies.
13. Health app > Profile > Health Records lists Geetha Health under Apps.
14. The user can turn off Geetha Health's access to any category here at any time.
15. If the user taps "Don't Share" in step 3 instead, Geetha Health receives no records. It shows "No health records found" and imports nothing.
